Frequently asked questions

Austin DME Supplier FAQs

How many DME supplier records are listed for Austin?

The CMS Provider Data dataset used by DMEvia contains 151 supplier records associated with Austin, Texas. This figure represents dataset records and should not be interpreted as 151 unique businesses.

What types of medical equipment can I explore?

Categories include CPAP equipment, oxygen equipment, wheelchairs, hospital beds, walkers, blood glucose supplies, nebulizers and patient lifts.

Can I search for suppliers near my ZIP code?

Yes. Use the DMEvia supplier directory to search using a ZIP code and an equipment category.

Is supplier information updated in real time?

No. The directory uses publicly available CMS provider data. Supplier details and participation may change. Contact suppliers directly to confirm current services, inventory and availability.

Does DMEvia endorse or verify individual suppliers?

No. Listing information is presented for discovery purposes only. DMEvia does not endorse individual suppliers or guarantee the accuracy or completeness of every record.
