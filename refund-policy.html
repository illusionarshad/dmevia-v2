Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains how cancellation and refund requests are handled for DMEvia Rule Radar subscriptions.

Effective date: November 26, 2026

We want subscription terms to be clear. Please review this policy before purchasing a Rule Radar subscription.

1. Cancelling a subscription

You may request cancellation of your Rule Radar subscription at any time. Unless the checkout or subscription terms presented to you state otherwise, cancellation stops the next renewal and your access continues until the end of the current paid billing period.

After the current paid period ends, subscription access will end. You will not be charged for a subsequent renewal after an effective cancellation.

2. Refunds for unused subscription time

Except where required by applicable law or expressly agreed by DMEvia, subscription fees are not automatically refunded or prorated for unused days remaining in a billing period after cancellation.

3. Duplicate charges and billing errors

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same subscription period, charged an incorrect amount, or charged after an effective cancellation, contact us. We will review the transaction and, where a billing error is verified, arrange an appropriate correction or refund.

4. Exceptional refund requests

You may contact us to request a review of an exceptional circumstance. Each request will be considered individually. A review does not guarantee that a refund will be granted.

5. How to request help

For cancellation assistance, billing questions or a refund review, email info@dmevia.com or use our Contact Us page.

Please include the email address associated with your DMEvia account, the relevant transaction date and a brief description of the issue. Do not send full payment-card numbers or security codes.

6. Payment processing

Payments may be processed by Paddle as DMEvia's payment provider or merchant of record, as applicable to the checkout presented to you. Payment receipts and transaction records may identify the payment processor. Refunds, where approved or legally required, are handled through the applicable payment-processing method.

7. Consumer rights and applicable law

This policy does not limit any mandatory rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under the laws applicable to you. If a mandatory legal requirement conflicts with this policy, the mandatory requirement will apply.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above indicates when the current version was published. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Important: This is a practical policy draft for DMEvia and is not a legal compliance certification. It should be checked against Paddle's current merchant requirements and applicable consumer-protection laws before live sales begin.
