FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about DME suppliers in Texas.

What is a DME supplier?

A durable medical equipment (DME) supplier provides medical equipment and related supplies used for medical purposes, including items such as wheelchairs, oxygen equipment, hospital beds and mobility aids.

How can I find a DME supplier in Texas?

Use DMEvia's supplier directory and enter your ZIP code to explore supplier listings. You can review available contact information and contact suppliers directly.

Does DMEvia sell medical equipment?

No. DMEvia is a supplier discovery platform. We help users find supplier information but do not sell, prescribe or distribute medical equipment.

Are all suppliers verified by DMEvia?

Supplier information is sourced from publicly available CMS data. Inclusion in the directory does not mean that DMEvia has independently inspected, endorsed or verified a supplier's current operations.

Should I confirm equipment availability with a supplier?

Yes. Contact the supplier directly to confirm current inventory, pricing, insurance acceptance, Medicare participation, delivery arrangements and other details.
