Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to you when you visit DMEvia.

Effective date: November 21, 2026

DMEvia ("DMEvia", "we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and handled when you visit or use the DMEvia website.

1. Information We Collect

DMEvia currently operates as an informational website and supplier discovery platform. We may receive or process the following categories of information:

Information you voluntarily provide. If you contact us by email, we may receive your name, email address, organisation or business name, the contents of your message, and any other information you choose to include.

Please do not send sensitive medical, financial or personal information unless it is genuinely necessary for your enquiry.

Supplier search information. Our DME supplier directory allows visitors to search using a US ZIP code and equipment category. The search uses a locally hosted dataset derived from publicly available CMS supplier information. The search is designed to operate within your browser. We do not ask you to submit medical information to search the directory.

Technical information. Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting infrastructure may process technical information associated with website visits, such as IP addresses, browser information and server logs, in accordance with the hosting provider's practices.

2. How We Use Information

Information voluntarily provided to us may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests.
  • Communicate with suppliers and business representatives.
  • Consider requests relating to supplier directory listings.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Protect the website and address technical or security issues.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

DMEvia does not currently use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or similar third-party advertising or visitor-tracking tools.

Our website hosting provider or third-party services accessed through the website may use technical mechanisms necessary to provide their services.

If we introduce analytics, advertising technologies or additional tracking features in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

4. Third-Party Services and External Links

Our website may connect visitors to third-party services, including GitHub Pages, which hosts our website; Titan, which provides our business email service; CMS public datasets, which provide supplier directory information; and external mapping services, which may open when visitors select a supplier map link.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. DMEvia does not control the privacy practices of external websites or services. We encourage visitors to review the relevant third-party policies when using those services.

5. Public Supplier Information

The DME supplier directory uses publicly available information from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). This information relates to supplier businesses and may include business names, addresses, telephone numbers and other directory information.

DMEvia does not create or independently verify the original CMS records. Information may be incomplete, inaccurate or outdated. Visitors should contact suppliers directly to confirm details.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information received through our website and business communications. However, no internet transmission, email communication or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Visitors should exercise appropriate caution when sharing information online.

7. Data Retention

We retain business correspondence and information voluntarily provided to us for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, maintain business records, resolve disputes or meet applicable obligations.

Technical records may be retained by our hosting or service providers under their respective policies.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details below. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

DMEvia is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services and business operations develop. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
